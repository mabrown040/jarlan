Brand Guide v1.0

Calcifer

The little fire demon that powers your financial independence. A brand built on warmth, clarity, and the magic of compound growth.

01 — Brand Essence

The Story We Tell

In Howl's Moving Castle, Calcifer is the fire demon who powers everything — the castle moves, the lights work, the water heats — all because of one small, bright flame. Your savings are your Calcifer. Small, persistent, and powerful enough to carry your entire life.

🔥

Warm, Not Corporate

We're the cozy hearth in a world of cold finance apps. Friendly, approachable, like a knowledgeable friend — not a suit.

🔬

Rigorous, Not Intimidating

We show our math. Every number is backed by real research. But we explain it in plain language, not academic jargon.

✨

Magical, Not Gimmicky

Studio Ghibli vibes: genuine wonder at compound interest, not fake hype. The magic is in the math — we just make it visible.

💪

Empowering, Not Preachy

We don't lecture about lattes. We give you the tools to make your own informed choices and see the real impact.

✓ Write like this

"Your portfolio survived 94% of historical periods. Here's what happened in the other 6%."

"At your current pace, you'll hit Coast FIRE in 3 years. Calcifer's feeling optimistic."

"The 4% rule was designed for 30-year retirements. You're planning for 50. Let's adjust."

✗ Never write like this

"Congratulations! You're on your way to financial freedom!!!"

"Stop buying coffee and you'll be rich."

"Our proprietary algorithm guarantees your retirement success."

03 — Typography

Three Voices

A display serif for headlines that feels like a storybook. A humanist sans-serif for body text that's warm and readable. A monospace for data that signals precision.

Display — DM Serif Display
Your FIRE Number
is $1,247,500
Use for: Headlines, page titles, large numbers, milestone announcements. The serif adds warmth and gravitas — this isn't a generic finance app, it's a story about your life.
Body — Plus Jakarta Sans
The Trinity Study analyzed rolling 30-year periods from 1926 to 1995 and found that a 4% initial withdrawal rate, adjusted annually for inflation, succeeded in the vast majority of scenarios. For early retirees planning 50+ year horizons, research from Early Retirement Now suggests 3.25–3.50% is more appropriate.
Use for: Body text, UI labels, navigation, form inputs. Weights 300–800 available. Default body: 400 weight, 1.05rem, 1.6 line-height.
Data — JetBrains Mono
